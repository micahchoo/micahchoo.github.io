Learn and look up

  • Skill Atlas

    Skill trees for real domains, drawn as a game's skill board. Pick a domain and walk its main quest.

  • Awesome Digital History: South Asia

    A curated list of South Asian digital archives, collections and histories of the digital.

  • DataViz for Indian Cities

    Urban mobility data for Pimpri-Chinchwad: vehicles, buses, traffic and road safety from 2000 to 2025.

  • Open Monosyllabic

    An explorer for one-syllable words that end in a vowel. Pick a sound shape such as /ma/ and see every language that has it.

  • Drum Rudiments Trainer

    All 40 drum rudiments on one practice pad: 3D hands, notation and a note lane, from 10 bpm up. Play along and the microphone scores you.

Make and annotate

  • Archie

    Annotate and publish your small archive. A multimedia exhibit platform that publishes as a static site.

  • Atlasdraw

    Draw on a map. An Excalidraw fork over MapLibre GL, with each shape anchored to a place.

  • Commonplace

    A notebook for the internet. Browse any public Are.na channel as a draggable notebook, fully static.

  • Better Writer

    You write. It asks you one question about what you just wrote, and never touches your words.
